# Where You Lead From — The Energy You Bring

IDEAL leadership reflection games. Both reveal the leadership energy you reach for first — Sovereign, Warrior, Lover/Connector or Jester — and the energy you may need to activate more deliberately.

| Game | Play it here | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Where You Lead From** | https://tanyl85-coder.github.io/where-you-lead-from/ | 8 situations, First Instinct + Second Move |
| **Energy Duels** (v2) | https://tanyl85-coder.github.io/where-you-lead-from/duels/ | 12 pairwise duels, one forced choice each |

Both are facilitated reflection exercises — not validated psychometric instruments.

## What happens to your answers

**Where You Lead From** keeps everything in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is collected or transmitted. The facilitator's room view aggregates result codes that participants share by hand, and unlocks at 5+ participants.

**Energy Duels** does the same by default. If you enter a **workshop code** on the welcome screen, one anonymous record is sent to that room's group report: your four energy scores, the tie-break winner if you had one, and your pressure tendency if you answered. Nothing else leaves your device — no name, no email, no individual duel choices, and not the commitment you write on the last screen. Leave the code box blank and nothing is sent at all.

The group report is aggregate only. It never shows names or individual results, and the database refuses to return anything until 5 people have finished under that code.

## For facilitators

Open the room view from the link on the welcome screen.

- **Energy Duels** — generate a workshop code, put it on screen, and the report builds itself as people finish. Includes lead energy distribution, average access rates, pressure tendencies, and print/PDF and CSV export. Setup: [`GROUP-REPORT-SETUP.md`](GROUP-REPORT-SETUP.md).
- **Where You Lead From**, or Energy Duels with no internet — collect the result codes participants show at the end and paste them in.
